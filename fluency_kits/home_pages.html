Dear Families,

Please spend 10 to 15 minutes each night on this packet. The plan on the next page tells you what to do each night. It is okay if some words are hard. A little struggle is how readers grow!

When your child gets stuck on a word

  1. Wait. Count to 5 in your head before you help.
  2. Say: “Tap the sounds.”
  3. Say: “Find the vowel. What sound does it make?”
  4. Say: “Cover part of the word. Read it in chunks.”
  5. Say: “Go back and read the whole sentence again.”
  6. Still stuck? Tell them the word, then have them reread the sentence.

Please do

  • Praise trying: “You kept going! That was hard and you did it.”
  • Let your child read to you, a pet, or a stuffed animal.
  • Use the pictures. They help!
  • Sign the reading log each night.

Please don’t

  • Read the whole story for them.
  • Give the word right away.
  • Worry about speed on Nights 1 and 2. Smooth comes first, then fast.

How to do a 1-Minute Read (Night 4)

  1. Set a timer for 1 minute. Your child starts at the top of the 1-Minute Read page.
  2. Lightly mark in pencil any word your child misses or skips.
  3. When the timer rings, mark the last word read. The number at the end of each line is the total words up to there. Count to the last word, then take away the missed words.
  4. Write the score and read again. Can they beat it? Smooth and correct matter more than fast.
